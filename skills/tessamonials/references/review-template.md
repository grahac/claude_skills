Omit fields not returned, or mark **unknown**. Minimize PII in the audience's copy.

## Findings
| Finding / interpretation | Distinct supporting interviews (n/N reviewed) | Evidence IDs / returned turn or position locator | Minimal exact quote or labeled paraphrase | Counterevidence / confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Distinguish a selected-review fraction from campaign-wide prevalence. Do not count
multiple mentions from one interview as multiple participants. Label unknown
population counts and overlapping themes. Never invent timestamps, locators, or
public interview URLs. If a transcript turn crosses pages, inspect the continuation
before quoting. Avoid personal contact details and personal invitation links.

approved or saved. Unapproval and withdrawal are not overridden by a positive review.
